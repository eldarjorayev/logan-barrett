About

Where Science
Meets Healing

Logan Barrett brings a rare interdisciplinary perspective to healthcare — one shaped by years of research at the intersection of music, psychology, and neuroscience. As Director of Behavioral Science and Research at Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center's Family Medicine Residency, he is advancing the program's educational and research mission with innovative, evidence-based approaches.

Prior to joining NFMMC, Logan held research positions at the University at Buffalo, Penn State University, and Durham University, where he led studies connecting auditory perception, cognition, and behavioral outcomes. He concurrently manages the Auditory Perception and Action Lab at the University at Buffalo.

Beyond research, Logan has served as a guest lecturer in sociological and psychological principles, a music director and board member for Lake Country Players in Watkins Glen, NY, and a community mentor across academic institutions.
